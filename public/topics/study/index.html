Study and annotations

Make evidence, comparison and personal work inspectable.

Study moves between passages, lexical resources, commentary, media and notes. Keep each source and anchor visible so the journey stays reversible.

Editorial design proposal102 · Study and annotations

Translations

From one translation chosen with enough context, through switching in place and verse-by-verse comparison, to parallel panes and the Greek or Hebrew under every word: decide how much of translation’s variety a reader sees, and keep them on the same verse while they look.

Research-informed design proposal103 · Study and annotations

Highlights and notes

From a bookmark that only says “return here”, through highlights and labelled marks, to private notes and notes shared with a group or the public: decide how much a mark says, who can see it, and what happens to it in another translation.

Research-informed design proposal104 · Study and annotations

Study helps

From a preview card under the verse, through a sheet and a study panel, to a page of its own or help only on request: decide where commentary, cross-references and sermon clips open, how readers find them, and how they get back to the verse.

Original design proposals with attributed sources, offered to everyone who builds technology for faith. We plant and water; God gives the growth, and no wireframe can promise it.
