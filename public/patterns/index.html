Sources and status

  • Observed

    An approach documented in a named app.

  • Familiar convention

    An established approach used across digital products.

  • Borrowed

    An approach adapted from another field, such as reading or productivity.

  • Exploratory

    A new design proposal awaiting user testing.

  • Leave it out

    Choosing to leave the feature out, a legitimate design decision.

  • Warning case

    Harmful behaviour found in products or research, shown so your team can recognise it.
