Pages waiting for review

Some pages touch the safety of people who may already be hurting. They are published as drafts, so that teams can use them now, and marked until people with the right expertise have reviewed them. If one of these is your field, or you know someone whose field it is, choose “Review or introduction” above.

  • Reading togetherThe verse turned against someone who shared, the leader’s notes and replies, the youth-group rules and the safeguarding contact in the working example. Needs: safeguarding; pastoral care; survivor advocacy (ideally survivor-led); and data protection. Offer a review
