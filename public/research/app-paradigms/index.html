Other kinds of apps

Further design questions. App examples reveal choices that deserve comparison, documentation and evaluation. Observations below are starting points for research, with their evidence type stated.

These observations inform design questions. They are not usability findings or newly validated patterns. Historical behaviour and documented behaviour need fresh testing before they guide a current implementation.

From observation to reusable guidance

  1. Capture the entry, action, resulting state and recovery on named devices and releases.
  2. Compare other implementations and identify the common task.
  3. Separate interface observation, provider claims and measured user outcomes.
  4. Describe alternatives and tradeoffs, then evaluate comprehension and control with relevant readers.
  5. Revise a pattern or add a new family when the decision is sufficiently distinct.
