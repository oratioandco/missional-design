A design reference

Research behind the decisions.

Caring well for people starts with knowing what is true. These notes keep Scripture and tradition, empirical findings, technical semantics, legal obligations and our own design proposals distinct, each with its limits.

Practical takeaways

  • Choose evidence by the claim: usability, learning, clinical outcomes and spiritual formation require different support.
  • Read source review limits before treating a documented feature as an effective intervention.
  • Use scenarios to test the complete journey, including recovery and service responsibility.
