Landscape · AI

How far AI reaches into the practice

Five steps, from search to generated prayer, and how far each product says it goes. 49 of 160 products state some use of AI. 27 go as far as generating prayers, devotions or voice; 4 stop at a companion or persona. 13 say their answers point to sources, and 7 state any human or theological review.

Questions that travel along the spectrum, from our research on digital spiritual formation and the waves of Bible and faith apps: can the person inspect sources, recognise uncertainty and reach human help? Does remembering serve formation, or only preference? Where does warm affirmation become an AI that agrees with everything?
