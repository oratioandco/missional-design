A design reference

Move from a focused passage to sustained reading

Support a bounded encounter without trapping the reader inside an excerpt.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

A devotion, shared practice or search opens a coherent passage page.

Proposed flow

  1. Orient

    Show full passage range, edition and editorial title.

  2. Read

    Keep the complete chosen unit accessible with optional audio or notes.

  3. Widen

    Open preceding or following context or continue through the book.

  4. Explore

    Move into reflection, comparison or a discussion only when chosen.

  5. Return

    Restore the focused passage and selected edition without discarding broader reading progress.

Recovery and alternative routes

Passages spanning chapters or differing editorial boundaries retain explicit references. Large text changes layout without losing the semantic anchor. A reader can choose continuous reading as their default.

What counts as a complete outcome

Both a short encounter and sustained reading remain complete options.
