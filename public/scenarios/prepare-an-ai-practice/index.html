A design reference

Prepare and participate in an AI-assisted practice

Use bounded adaptation while keeping Scripture, authorship and user choice inspectable.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

The person requests a devotional, meditation or guided reflection.

Proposed flow

  1. Set scope

    Choose passage, length, interpretive scope and purpose; personal disclosure is optional.

  2. Choose production

    Offer authored content, reviewed template adaptation or an inspectable generated draft.

  3. Review

    Check transcript and sources before spoken playback; allow edits or an authored alternative.

  4. Participate

    Choose listening only, optional questions or a dialogue branch.

  5. Close

    Return from branches, finish freely and decide separately what to save.

Recovery and alternative routes

Generation failure retains any verified text and offers stable alternatives. Regeneration creates a visible new version. Sensitive disclosures use the care route rather than an automatic personalised meditation.

What counts as a complete outcome

The user knows how content was produced and can finish without handing over personal data.

Decisions within this flow

79 · AI agency and care

Identify what AI did, where it matters

Label the actor, action and artifact at the moment they could be misunderstood.

Research-informed design proposal

Engagement facets to specify

Possible situations: Personal devotion, Encounter and curiosity. These are overlapping task contexts, not fixed user categories.

Specify the chosen purpose, passage unit, pace, participation, social setting, interpretive task, environment and disclosure or teaching responsibility. Use the scenario framework.
