A design reference

Return after a break without debt

Support an irregular rhythm and a manageable next session.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

A person returns after missed sessions or changes in life circumstances.

Proposed flow

  1. Orient

    Offer the saved place and something useful for today.

  2. Choose rhythm

    Resume, reschedule, reduce frequency or pause.

  3. Choose feedback

    Show factual activity only if useful; hide streaks or badges.

  4. Practise

    Allow a small session, a deeper study or quiet participation.

  5. Finish

    Complete without catching up, posting or disclosing a reflection.

Recovery and alternative routes

Reminders remain adjustable and shared participation is voluntary. Missed sessions do not trigger guilt or monetised streak repair.

What counts as a complete outcome

The plan fits the person rather than the person owing the plan.
