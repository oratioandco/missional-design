A design reference

Follow the calendar into prayer

Open a named observance and participate in an appropriate prayer practice.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

Today’s view or a date shared by a community.

Proposed flow

  1. Confirm context

    Identify calendar, date, region and service.

  2. Open

    Show sourced readings or prayer, with alternatives explained.

  3. Participate

    Choose text or supported audio, with instructions and responses clear.

  4. Pause

    Preserve the chosen date and section rather than replacing it at midnight.

  5. Finish

    Allow an ending without recording completion or sharing personal content.

Recovery and alternative routes

Missing calendar data is disclosed. Offer cached material only with its date and version clear. A return to today is an explicit action.

What counts as a complete outcome

The person can follow the chosen practice and understand where its material came from.
