A design reference

Receive prayer support without hidden profiling

Choose useful words or silence while retaining ownership of intentions.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

The person asks for a prayer or opens a prayer experience.

Proposed flow

  1. Choose form

    Offer attributed authored prayer, an editable draft or silence.

  2. Choose disclosure

    Only use intentions deliberately supplied for this task.

  3. Review wording

    Remove assumptions and adjust language or tradition.

  4. Read or listen

    Playback does not request microphone access; voice input is separate.

  5. Finish

    Save, share or set a reminder only through distinct choices.

Recovery and alternative routes

A refusal of personalisation still leads to a complete experience. Sensitive details must not become hidden memories, public summaries or notification previews.

What counts as a complete outcome

No promise of answered prayer, divine authorship or exclusive AI relationship.

Decisions within this flow

79 · AI agency and care

Identify what AI did, where it matters

Label the actor, action and artifact at the moment they could be misunderstood.

Research-informed design proposal

Engagement facets to specify

Possible situations: Contemplation and prayer, Doubt, lament and life questions. These are overlapping task contexts, not fixed user categories.

Specify the chosen purpose, passage unit, pace, participation, social setting, interpretive task, environment and disclosure or teaching responsibility. Use the scenario framework.
