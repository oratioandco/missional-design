A design reference

Listen, pause narration and repeat

Create a bounded listening-and-reflection session.

Design proposal · User validation required
Entry

Selected passage or chapter, edition and recording.

Proposed flow

  1. Configure

    Choose narration, optional ambience and repeat unit; check timing support.

  2. Listen

    Show playback reference independently from reading position.

  3. Pause narration

    Choose music or silence, and manual or visible timed resumption.

  4. Repeat

    Use a finite count or deliberately chosen continuous loop with an explicit gap.

  5. Finish

    Stop all audio or resume once; preserve the listening anchor.

Recovery and alternative routes

Calls, offline media, sleep timers and source changes recheck the session state. No verse timing means a track-level loop, not estimated precision.

What counts as a complete outcome

The session can end without autoplay or a further disclosure.
