Prototypes

Some ideas are easier to judge once you can touch them. This page holds creative interface experiments: unfinished, sometimes playful, and always labelled as exploration rather than recommendation.

Experiments

  • Rocks that cry out

    A logo study: a silicon rock, wider at the base, that slowly turns and throws a cross-shaped glint when a facet catches the light. After Luke 19:40, the Berlin TV tower’s “Pope’s revenge” and the star filters of photographers.

    Open the experiment →

  • A passage and a song

    The morning devotional format from Aperto: about five minutes that open the day with one bounded passage and one song written from the same exegetical work. What can a song carry of the text, and what should stay in the reading?

    Open the experiment →

  • Listening grounded in the text

    Experiments from Aperto, where generated devotional audio stays tied to the passage and the commentary behind it. Mary’s song, Luke 1:46–56, as the worked example.

    Open the experiment →

  • A rosary that keeps time

    Beads, rhythm and motion for a traditional Catholic prayer, including a rapped version. When does animation help someone pray, and when does it become a performance to watch?

    An idea, not built yet

  • One field for every kind of search

    A study of the combined passage field in the Video Study Bible prototype: book filtering, reference recognition and AI suggestions in one place, and how to keep them distinguishable.

    An idea, not built yet

  • A devotional for a run

    An upbeat audio devotional paced to a run or a walk. What changes when Scripture, music and movement share the same few minutes?

    An idea, not built yet

The two Aperto experiments study one working, author-associated product; the remaining ideas are not built yet. Each experiment states what it explores and what it does not establish.
