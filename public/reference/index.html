What good work makes possible

More room for life beyond the screen.

A welcoming restart, an answer people can examine, a conversation that ends well: design choices can strengthen discernment and relationships. Continued use can support a meaningful practice; it is not, by itself, evidence of spiritual growth.

The calling

“Listen—if they were silent, the very rocks would start to shout!”

Luke 19:40, The Voice™ · Copyright © 2008 by Ecclesia Bible Society. Used by permission. All rights reserved.

Jesus said this when he was told to silence the crowds who were praising him. Making him known is entrusted to us first.

But many people now talk to rocks first. A microchip is a rock we tricked into thinking, and with AI the rock talks back: people ask it about God before they ask anyone else.

Those of us who build on these rocks, and shape how they behave, get to help them reflect Christ and lead people into deeper knowledge of God and life with his people. It is an exhilarating calling, and also a burden, and this resource is here to help you carry it well.

About the options

Sources and status

The labels identify each option’s source and development status.

  • Observed

    An approach documented in a named app.

  • Familiar convention

    An established approach used across digital products.

5-minute route

Context around the passage

How much context does a reader need around the passage? Choosing the reading unit, landing in the right place, and keeping Scripture, notes and explanations distinct.

What does this person need to understand or find?5-minute route

Trust in AI answers

What authority does a fluent answer seem to have? Giving AI a bounded job, showing what it did, letting people check its sources, and keeping care in human hands.

Finding passages

How does someone find the passage they are looking for? Reader controls, browsing, typed references, combined search fields and book order, as one journey into Scripture.
