A Resource Guide panel below the text

The reader shows Matthew 1:1 in the NGÜ at the top, with a panel below headed “Mat 1:1-6”. The panel shows a Commentary Select promotion and “Related Verses” in five Bibles with counts (ESV 5, NKJV 5, NASB 3, NLT 1). Tabs at the bottom read Resource Guide, Parallel, Notes and Lookup.

Design takeaway

A panel that follows the passage keeps resources current as the reader moves. Say which verses the panel is showing, as the “Mat 1:1-6” header does, and separate promotions from the resources themselves.

Study helps

From a preview card under the verse, through a sheet and a study panel, to a page of its own or help only on request: decide where commentary, cross-references and sermon clips open, how readers find them, and how they get back to the verse.
