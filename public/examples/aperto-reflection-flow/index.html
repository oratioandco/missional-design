Aperto · Direct local prototype capture

Reflection setup and an observed generation failure

A synthetic gratitude intention leads to feeling and companion-style choices, then requested-support choices. Grateful and Gratitude were selected. Beginning generation returned “Generation failed”; the choices, Back, Skip and Begin remained visible.

Design takeaway

Separate optional disclosure, requested support and companion style. Preserve the visible setup after failure, explain recovery, and test a route that does not require personal disclosure.

What this example establishes

A synthetic gratitude intention leads to feeling and companion-style choices, then requested-support choices. Grateful and Gratitude were selected. Beginning generation returned “Generation failed”; the choices, Back, Skip and Begin remained visible.

Limits and further verification

No meditation was successfully generated in this attempt. Retry, skip, saved-state persistence and completed audio were not verified. This is a development prototype, not a claim about the released product.
