Aperto · Local prototype capture · synthetic session · cropped

Named feelings, companion style and a skip route

The step presents feeling choices, four companion styles and a visible skip action. The Continue action is disabled before a feeling is selected.

Design takeaway

Separate optional personalisation from the ability to begin. A feeling chosen by a person should not silently become a diagnosis or a durable psychological profile.

What this example establishes

The step presents feeling choices, four companion styles and a visible skip action. The Continue action is disabled before a feeling is selected.

Limits and further verification

The skip control is visible; its resulting generation was not exercised. The UI does not establish whether selections are stored or how style changes model behaviour.
