Monthly gifts as Bibles per year

“Become a Sower and take the Bible to the world.” (the account holder’s first name before it is blurred here), a video, and a slider of monthly amounts from $10 to $100: at $25, “600 Bibles Per Year”. A green “Give a Monthly Gift” button stays on screen.

Design takeaway

Translating money into outcomes helps people see what they give to. Say what “a Bible” means here and where the figure comes from.
