Dwell · User-contributed screenshot · resized, content unchanged

The current verse bright, the rest dimmed

In Dwell’s Listen view, Genesis 1:2 is in full white while verses 1, 3 and 4 above and below are dimmed, each with its reference underneath. A Listen and Read switch sits at the top and a player shows “Genesis 1 · 31 Verses”.

Design takeaway

Focus with the context still visible keeps the place during audio without hiding the passage. Check that dimmed text is still readable and announced by screen readers.
