Mere Bible (formerly NeuBible) · User-contributed screenshot · unedited

Translations in a sheet over the reader

A Change Version sheet lists abbreviations and full translation names. Some rows display a cloud-download symbol, while the chapter remains dimly visible behind the sheet.

Design takeaway

Distinguish choosing an edition from downloading it. Explain availability in text or accessible labels rather than relying on a cloud icon alone.
