Two editions with independent selectors

Opening Parallel adds a second pane. Its edition selector was changed from KJV to WEBUS while the first pane stayed KJV. Each pane has an edition label and a close action.

Design takeaway

Give each edition a visible identity and a clear way to leave comparison. A passage URL can encode the secondary edition as well as the primary reference.

What this example establishes

Opening Parallel adds a second pane. Its edition selector was changed from KJV to WEBUS while the first pane stayed KJV. Each pane has an edition label and a close action.

Limits and further verification

Opening comparison and changing the secondary edition were observed. Synchronised scrolling, position restoration and mobile layouts were not tested.
