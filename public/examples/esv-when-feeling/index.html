Audio playlists by feeling

The Explore sheet’s Playlists tab shows a “When I’m Feeling” shelf with “When I’m Feeling Lonely” (17 tracks), “Anxious” (24) and “Overwhelmed” (22), between Joy, Peace and Confidence above and Holidays & Special Occasions below. Some covers carry a lock icon.

Design takeaway

Feeling-based entry gives someone without a reference a way in. Say who curated each playlist and keep the passages connected to their context.
