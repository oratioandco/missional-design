“Pick an audio length”

A sheet over a session titled “Take Courage” offers four lengths: “2 min · Pause & Pray”, “5 min · Pause & Renew”, “10 min · Meditate & Reflect” and “15 min · Meditate & Deepen”. Behind it, Exodus 3:11–12 is shown.

Design takeaway

Offering lengths respects the listener’s day. Check that the short versions still read the passage whole, and say what is left out.

Devotional audio

From guided prayer with Scripture read twice and room for silence, through a teaching conversation and a one-voice devotional talk, to a sleep story that fades out, and an endless generated stream as a warning case: decide what each format does with Scripture, with the listener’s attention, and with the end of the session.

Ending the conversation

From an assistant that closes well, through a suggested break and a length the person chooses, to a chat that never ends, a reply that always offers one more thing, a hook at goodbye and guilt to keep someone talking: decide how a conversation with the assistant ends, and whose good the ending serves.
