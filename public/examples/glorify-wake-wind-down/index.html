“Wake up” and “Wind down” reminders

Two reminders, “WAKE UP” at 8:00 AM and “WIND DOWN” at 10:00 PM, each listing the days and with its own switch (both off), above the line “Set different times for your Daily Worship on weekdays and weekends to create good habits fast.” and an “ADD NEW REMINDER” button.

Design takeaway

Reminders the person times to their own morning and evening. “Good habits fast” frames prayer as habit-building; say instead what the time is for.
