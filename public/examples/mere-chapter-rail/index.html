Mere Bible (formerly NeuBible) · User-contributed screenshot · unedited

Chapter navigation beside the reading text

A vertical numbered chapter rail appears at the right of Deuteronomy while the reading text and separate passage, WEB and Aa controls remain visible. Another supplied frame shows a large 25 indicator while the reader still names chapter 17.

Design takeaway

Distinguish the current chapter from a candidate destination during scrubbing. Preserve a deliberate way to dismiss the rail and a larger-target alternative. Do not assume a still image establishes when navigation commits.
