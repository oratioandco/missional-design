Spark Bible · User-contributed screenshot · resized, content unchanged

An AI label and thumbs at the end of the results

A numbered list of results for “Talking animals” (Revelation 5:13, Psalm 104:25-26, Matthew 7:6…) ends with “These results are generated with the assistance of a specially-tuned AI model. Your feedback helps us improve these results:” and thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons. A “Plus” upsell banner sits above.

Design takeaway

Labelling AI help where the results end is honest; say what happens to the feedback, and keep upsells apart from the label so one is not read as the other.

Saying “I don’t know”

From an assistant that answers everything with the same confidence, through a warning in the fine print and a confidence level on each answer, to answers that say which parts are Scripture, which are a tradition’s teaching and which are a guess, an honest “I don’t know”, and declining to answer: decide how the assistant shows the limits of what it knows.
