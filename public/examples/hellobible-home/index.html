HelloBible · Publisher promotional mockup

A daily verse card alongside practice entry points

The mockup combines a daily verse with reference and actions, service cards, a prominent central action, and a numerical flame indicator.

Design takeaway

A home screen can connect a short Scripture encounter with further practices. Explain progress indicators without implying that a number measures faith or wellbeing.

What this example establishes

The mockup combines a daily verse with reference and actions, service cards, a prominent central action, and a numerical flame indicator.

Limits and further verification

This is a promotional mockup, not a live app capture. The flame’s precise meaning, central action, chat output, storage behaviour and safeguarding responses were not tested.
