Lumen Bible · Direct signed-out web capture

Optional choices about engagement and experience

An optional onboarding step offers curiosity or skepticism, beginning or returning, growth, and deeper study. A Skip control is visible; using it opened the home screen without choosing a faith category.

Design takeaway

Offer a low-pressure way to state current intent. Experience, belief and task depth are separate facets; do not turn one onboarding answer into a permanent spiritual ranking.

What this example establishes

An optional onboarding step offers curiosity or skepticism, beginning or returning, growth, and deeper study. A Skip control is visible; using it opened the home screen without choosing a faith category.

Limits and further verification

Necessary-only service choices were used. Subsequent personalised output, preference persistence, accessibility and mobile behaviour were not evaluated. The labels are the provider’s categories, not validated user segments.
