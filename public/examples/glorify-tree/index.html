Progress as a growing tree

“My Journey” shows a sapling with roots, “Tree progress 3/10” as a segmented bar, “Trees grown 1”, and current and longest streaks of 0 and 2.

Design takeaway

A metaphor can make small progress feel alive, and starts small enough for beginners. Here it sits beside a streak of 0, which still makes the gap visible.
