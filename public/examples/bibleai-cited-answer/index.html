A “Predicted Answer” with numbered sources

For “Talking animals”, a box labelled “Predicted Answer*” explains Balaam’s donkey and the serpent with circled numbers in the text. Chips below read “1 Genesis 3:1”, “2 Numbers 22:21-30” and “11 Desiring God”, above tabs for passages, articles, books and videos.

Design takeaway

Numbered markers connect claims to sources and mix Scripture with a ministry’s articles. Show each source’s type, and check that every marker supports the sentence it sits in.

How human the assistant seems

From a plain tool with no name, through labelled and named assistants, to personas, companions and chatbots speaking as Jesus: decide how much personhood an AI in a faith app performs, and make what it is as clear as how it sounds.

Research-informed design proposal124 · Conversation and content

Saying “I don’t know”

From an assistant that answers everything with the same confidence, through a warning in the fine print and a confidence level on each answer, to answers that say which parts are Scripture, which are a tradition’s teaching and which are a guess, an honest “I don’t know”, and declining to answer: decide how the assistant shows the limits of what it knows.
