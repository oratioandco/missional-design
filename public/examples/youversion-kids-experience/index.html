A Kids Bible Experience card in the Today feed

In the Today feed, between a reading plan and other cards, a card reads “He is Holy” with “Kids Bible Experience” and a photo of children. The account holder’s photo is blurred.

Design takeaway

Children’s content appears in the adult feed, for a parent to share. A child using a parent’s phone sees everything else in the feed too.
