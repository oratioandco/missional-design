Book list with history, audio markers and two orders

The Books list (German interface) has Cancel and History at the top, a speaker icon and an expand arrow on every book row, and a Traditional or Alphabetical switch at the bottom.

Design takeaway

Offering both orders helps people who know a book’s name but not its place. Label what the speaker icon means in text, not only as an icon, and keep History distinct from browsing.
