VSB · Direct Android prototype capture

A selection survives returning from its study sheet

Returning from the expanded study sheet leaves verse 18 highlighted. The floating reader control shows a different visible passage position, while the collapsed study handle retains the selected reference and video count.

Design takeaway

Model visible position, selection and study context as separate anchors. Preserve selection on return and make the active study reference explicit, especially before switching editions or navigating elsewhere.

What this example establishes

Returning from the expanded study sheet leaves verse 18 highlighted. The floating reader control shows a different visible passage position, while the collapsed study handle retains the selected reference and video count.

Limits and further verification

Returning with Android Back was observed. Subsequent scrolling, translation changes, persistence across sessions and large-text settings were not tested. No personal conversation is shown. Scripture: Lutherbibel 2017; edition rights remain with its publisher.
