A question for the group at the end of a unit

“Einheit 1 / 6” (unit 1 of 6) mixes short teaching with John 3:16 in bold and its reference in red. Near the end: “Damit kommen wir zur letzten Frage:” (that brings us to the last question), then a red banner: “Welchen Fragen stellst Du an Dein Leben?” (what questions do you ask of your life?).

Design takeaway

A question gives a group something to talk about. Say who will see each answer, and let people answer only if they want.
