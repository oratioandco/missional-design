A profile of series, time in the app and conversations

The profile shows a week strip under “Series” with days 5 to 8 marked, then six tiles: Current series 1 day, Daily verses 0, Record series 1 day, Time in the app 5 min, Conversations 32, Time Today 0 min. Settings follow; none of them lists or edits the answers given during onboarding.

Design takeaway

Counting time in the app and conversations measures the product’s engagement, not the person’s life with God. If onboarding answers personalise the app, let people see and change them here.
