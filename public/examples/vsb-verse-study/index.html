VSB · Direct Android prototype capture

A verse sheet combines media evidence and a summary

The sheet identifies a verse and edition, lists two video contributions with titles and reference ranges, and presents a summary with numbered source markers. It labels the summary as AI-generated and potentially fallible.

Design takeaway

Keep Scripture, source media and generated interpretation recognisably separate. Give each contribution a route back to its source rather than presenting a synthesis as the Bible text.

What this example establishes

The sheet identifies a verse and edition, lists two video contributions with titles and reference ranges, and presents a summary with numbered source markers. It labels the summary as AI-generated and potentially fallible.

Limits and further verification

The installed prototype state was captured directly. Video playback, timestamp accuracy and summary grounding were not evaluated. No personal conversation is shown. Interface language is German. Scripture: Lutherbibel 2017; media imagery retains its respective rights.
