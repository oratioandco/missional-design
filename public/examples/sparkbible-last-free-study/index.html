“You just used your last free study”

Over an AI-generated “Themes - Psalm 23” sheet, a dialog reads “Get Unlimited AI-Enhanced Study Tools!” and “You just used your last free study, but you can unlock more by starting your free trial of Spark Bible Plus!”, with Learn More and Maybe later.

Design takeaway

A free allowance for AI tools reflects a real cost per answer. Say what each study costs to make, show the allowance before it runs out, and keep crisis help outside any limit.
