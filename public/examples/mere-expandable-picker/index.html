Mere Bible (formerly NeuBible) · User-contributed screenshot · unedited

Book list with an inline chapter grid

Deuteronomy is expanded within the Books list. Its 34 chapters appear as a grid, chapter 25 is coloured, and neighbouring book rows remain in the same list.

Design takeaway

An accordion preserves book-list context while revealing chapters. Plan for long grids, keeping the selected chapter visible, and restoring position when a book collapses.
