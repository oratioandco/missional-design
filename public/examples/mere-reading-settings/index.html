Mere Bible (formerly NeuBible) · User-contributed screenshot · unedited

Reading settings in a bottom drawer

A settings drawer covers the lower reader while a dimmed passage remains above it. It exposes Light and Dark, font selection, a text-size slider, three spacing icons, and switches for Red Letter, Verse Numbers, Note Indicators and Section Headings.

Design takeaway

Keep preferences in context and separate typography from content-layer visibility. Label spacing choices accessibly; preserve the reading anchor after reflow. Hiding an indicator should not delete its underlying note.
