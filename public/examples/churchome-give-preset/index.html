A church’s give screen with $100.00 filled in

“THE CHURCHOME APP IS FREE TO ALL THANKS TO THE GENEROSITY OF PEOPLE LIKE YOU.” Below, “Gift Amount $100.00” is already filled in, above “Give with PushPay”, “Give with Apple Pay”, “Give with PayPal” and “Recurring Giving”. A guest banner reads “You are currently logged in as a guest.”

Design takeaway

Saying that gifts keep the app free is honest. A filled-in amount decides for the giver, and $100 is a large first suggestion for someone new; start with none.
