Lumen Bible · Direct signed-out web capture

Phrase-grouped text inside a chapter reader

The reader shows a complete chapter with section headings and phrase-grouped lines. Study, comparison and read-aloud entry points sit above the text. A verse-inspection hint was dismissed before capture.

Design takeaway

Compare phrase grouping with continuous prose for orientation, line tracking and understanding. A section heading can structure reading without making the chapter an isolated verse list.
