Bookmark and Share on each passage card

Search results appear as passage cards (Job 12:7, Job 38:41, Psalm 104:25-26, Isaiah 34:14), each with its own Bookmark and Share actions.

Design takeaway

Putting actions on the Scripture card means the passage, not generated text, is what people save and pass on.

What this example establishes
