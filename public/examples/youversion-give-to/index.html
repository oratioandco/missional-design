“Give to”: the app, or Bible translation

A sheet titled “Give to” offers YouVersion (“When you support YouVersion, you get to help others experience God’s generous love”) and Bible Translation (“Help us get the Bible translated into every language by the year 2033.”).

Design takeaway

Naming two purposes lets people choose what their gift pays for. Each still needs to say who receives the money and what it covers.
