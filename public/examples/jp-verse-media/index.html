Teaching cards pinned to a verse

In Genesis 1 (NKJV, verse mode), a swipeable row of cards sits between verses 1 and 2. The first, marked “Genesis 1:1”, reads “See Jesus’ signature revealed from the very beginning of creation · Video · 2 Mins” beside a photo of the preacher and a lock badge; the next card, “A picture of the menorah hidden in Genesis 1:1 · Image”, peeks from the right, with three dots below.

Design takeaway

Media anchored to the exact verse puts teaching where the question arises. It also places one teacher’s voice, and a subscription lock, inside the reading of Scripture.
