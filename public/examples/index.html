Coverage to expand

Priorities for further captures include further native VSB flows, current mobile YouVersion and Dwell releases, interlinear readers, audio timing settings and complete conversational journeys. No screenshot or behaviour claim is implied for an app or state that has not been captured.

How to use this collection

Read the observation before drawing conclusions from an image. Follow its source for publisher context and use the linked patterns to compare alternatives. An account state, device, language, edition or release may change the experience.

The collection is selective and evolving. Further releases, platforms, accessibility settings and complete flows can add evidence or change an interpretation. Capture entries are dated individually rather than presented as timeless product descriptions.

Publisher images remain hosted by their publishers and may change or disappear; each entry retains its source link. Interface and image rights remain with the respective rights holders. Attribution does not grant a general reuse licence.
