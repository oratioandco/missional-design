Pray.com · User-contributed screenshot · resized, content unchanged

Praise and Remind buttons for other people’s streaks

A Community Board lists “Active Streakers” with day counts in the thousands and Praise buttons, the viewer as “You · 1 days”, and a “Remind” section with Remind buttons beside people at 7, 2 and 1 days. “Invite to Pray” asks for access to contacts.

Design takeaway

A board makes each person’s prayer record visible to others and invites nudging those who missed a day. Prayer “in secret” needs no audience; if a group prays together, share intentions by choice, never records.
