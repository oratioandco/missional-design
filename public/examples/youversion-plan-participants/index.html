Progress and participants on each plan

My Plans lists three plans. “Bible on Business” shows a short progress bar and “1 Participant” with a small avatar (blurred here); the others show a cover and a title.

Design takeaway

Progress and people sit together on the plan itself. In a plan with friends everyone sees each other’s completed days; say so before people join, and keep the bar for the reader alone unless they choose to show it.
