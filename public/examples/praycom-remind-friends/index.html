Pray.com · User-contributed screenshot · resized; names and photos of other users blurred

“Active Streakers” to praise, and friends to “Remind”

A “Community Board” lists “Active Streakers” with the number of days each has kept a streak and a Praise button, then a “Remind” list of friends who stopped one to seven days ago, each with a Remind button, and “Invite to Pray” with access to contacts. Names and photos are blurred here.

Design takeaway

A button to remind a friend who stopped turns one person’s silence into another’s notification. If friends are part of the app, let them pray for each other, not prompt each other back.
