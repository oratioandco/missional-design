Compact list or expanded annotation cards

The publisher compares compact reference rows with expanded verse cards. The accompanying release article describes a toggle and remembered view preference.

Design takeaway

Offer density choices when people alternate between finding a saved item and reading its contents. Preserve the preference without changing the saved content.
