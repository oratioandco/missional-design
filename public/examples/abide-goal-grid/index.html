“What brings you here today?” as a photo grid

A multi-select grid of twelve photo tiles (Reduce Anxiety, Grow Spiritually, Feel Peace, Sleep Better, Strengthen Relationships, Connect With God, Trust God, Experience Freedom, Increase Hope, Receive Healing, Improve Character, Love Well) under “Select one or more to continue.” Earlier steps ask for content types; between steps, interstitials show listener counts and five-star testimonials before a subscription offer.

Design takeaway

Goals can shape a first suggestion. Say what each choice changes, let people skip, and keep social proof and sales out of the questions themselves.

Check-ins and assessments

From a few setup questions that only shape what the app suggests, through a daily check-in, a gifts inventory and a church survey, to a spiritual score as a warning case: decide what each question is for, what is kept, who sees a result, and how much the result claims to know.

First use

From opening on a passage, a prayer or a question box with nothing asked, through a first page of places to start, guest use and a short tour, to questions first and payment before the first use: decide what a new person must give, and when, before they reach what they came for.
