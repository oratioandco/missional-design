Context around the passage

How much context does a reader need around the passage? Choosing the reading unit, landing in the right place, and keeping Scripture, notes and explanations distinct.

Listening

Can the practice continue when the screen goes dark? Screen-off listening, read-along, chosen silence and an ending that lets the phone disappear.

Trust in AI answers

What authority does a fluent answer seem to have? Giving AI a bounded job, showing what it did, letting people check its sources, and keeping care in human hands.

What authority might a fluent answer appear to have?8-minute guide

Attention and time

What would “enough for now” look like? Reminders, emails, feeds and endings: asking for attention across every channel without taking more than was offered.

Pricing and access

Who pays for a faith app, and for what? What a Bible or faith app really costs, how ministries and businesses pay for it, and where the money can strain trust.

What is being paid for, by whom, and what happens when the money stops?8-minute guide

Analytics

What is the least you need to know? Choosing metrics and research methods that answer product questions while protecting chats, prayers and reading histories.

Community and visibility

Who can see this, respond to it, and hold authority? Choosing the audience, accountability and moderation for shared plans, prayer requests, discussion and mentoring before adding a feed.

Finding passages

How does someone find the passage they are looking for? Reader controls, browsing, typed references, combined search fields and book order, as one journey into Scripture.
