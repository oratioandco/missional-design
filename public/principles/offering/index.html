Missional by Design · The makers

Make it well, as an offering

Craft is worship. Excellence, accessibility, beauty and care are how we love God and the person on the other side of the screen.

From the calling

  • God fills craftspeople with his Spirit
  • Craft turns the facets towards the light

Scripture and tradition

“I have filled him with the Spirit of God, with skill, ability, and knowledge in all kinds of craftsmanship” (Exodus 31:3). “Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, as for the Lord and not for men” (Colossians 3:23). The same skill can serve an idol: Aaron fashioned the calf “with an engraving tool” (Exodus 32:4).

Praxis calls the redemptive way “creative restoration through sacrifice”: making new things that “create possibility and opportunity for others”. faith.tools asks whether an app is “maintained and regularly updated”, and asks of every app: does it “properly cite and license the Bible translations it uses”?

Sources: What is redemptive entrepreneurship? · faith.tools selection criteria

What it rules in, and out

In

  • A reader that is a joy to read in, and typography that honours the text
  • Accessibility as standard: screen readers, contrast, dyslexia, other languages
  • Stillness, beauty and reliability
  • Translations cited and licensed

Out

  • “It’s for ministry” as an excuse for careless work
  • Beauty used as bait

What the maker gives up. Shipping fast at the cost of the people who cannot use what was shipped.

Qualification. Excellence is not luxury. A small team can make a simple thing well; what matters is care for the person, not polish for the portfolio.

Test a design

Would we offer this to God as it is, and could the person with the least sight, time or reading ease use it?

  • What did we leave unfinished that someone else will have to carry?
