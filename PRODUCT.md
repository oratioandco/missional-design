## Users

Christian technologists, product designers and builders, alongside church/ministry leaders and Christian nonprofit/business teams. The owner explicitly wants the work to speak to the audience of FaithTech and Praxis, with comparable visual craft and accessible communication. First-time visitors have no knowledge of the project's development history.

## Product Purpose

Connect Christian mission, contextual discernment and practical design. Central question: “What really is the good news in our context?” Rapid change means renewed listening and recontextualization are continuous tasks even without crossing a border. Help teams understand the perspective, discern their context and work through concrete design decisions.

## Positioning

Missional identity/context canvas, theological product principles, and a source-aware Bible/faith pattern reference belong to one approach. The gospel is not a commercial value proposition; usage or growth alone does not demonstrate spiritual formation. Design should strengthen discernment, agency and relationships with God and other people.

## Operating Context

Church/team workshops, product reviews, individual research and sustained reading. Resources work on mobile and desktop and extend to PDFs. Visitors move from an accessible introduction to an applied example, principles, canvas or patterns, with source reasoning available in depth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Church canvas with Christian nonprofit and business draft adaptations; browser-local workbook and save/reopen.
- Filled export is ONE A3 landscape page in the actual nine-block spatial layout with all guiding questions and full answers inside each block. Preserve overflow safeguards and local data.
- Pattern options, working demos, source records, Scripture/quote verification and specialist-review status are distinct. Do not turn research hypotheses into established findings.
- Private iPhone app corpus stays excluded from publication. No fabricated testimonials, endorsements or spiritual outcomes.
- English/German main pages; much of the reference and current canvas remain English. State that clearly.

## Brand Commitments

Owner confirmed 16 October: the rock metaphor is key and the approved rock visual must remain; the rest of the visual identity may evolve/change. Approved specimen is brand/assets/silicon-context-v7.webp in the adjacent reference checkout, derived from the approved neighborhood film. Broad rugged fractures, reflective icy blue-silver sheen and optical reflected-light glints, no cross cutout. It expresses reflected witness, not the foundation or the gospel itself.

Photography should retain urban/startup editorial-commercial colour, human presence and subtle analog qualities. Fictional generated people/scenes remain labelled. Familiar metal icon shapes can have slight surface irregularity; do not build icons from abstract rocks. Previous preferences include Instrument Serif and a two-rule “by” wordmark; typography and broader palette are now open to considered evolution. Avoid corny biblical props, generic templates and a teal-dominant reset.
